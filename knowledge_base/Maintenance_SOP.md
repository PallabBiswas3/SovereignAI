# Maintenance SOP 12 — Rotating Equipment

Classification: Internal  
Department: Maintenance

[PAGE 1]

## Section 7.4 Vibration Limits

For centrifugal process pumps in continuous operation, overall vibration velocity measured at the bearing housing shall not exceed 6.0 mm/s RMS. Readings above 6.0 mm/s require maintenance intervention. Readings above 9.0 mm/s require the pump to be removed from service pending engineering review.

[PAGE 2]

## Section 7.5 Bearing Temperature

Normal bearing temperature is up to 80 °C. A temperature from 80 °C through 90 °C requires planned maintenance and increased monitoring. A temperature above 90 °C requires shutdown and inspection.

[PAGE 3]

## Section 7.6 Discharge Pressure

Pump-102 normal discharge pressure is 4.8 to 5.5 bar. Pressure outside this range requires process and mechanical checks before continued duty.

[PAGE 4]

## Section 8.2 Replacement Decision

Replacement should be considered only when inspection confirms irreparable casing damage, repeated critical vibration after overhaul, or repair cost exceeding 60 percent of replacement cost. A single abnormal reading by itself does not establish a replacement requirement.
